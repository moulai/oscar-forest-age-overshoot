# Prepared-input methods and source routines

The study can be reproduced from the exact prepared artifacts listed in `inputs/catalogue.json`; raw-raster reduction is not performed at model runtime. The input-preparation routines and their direct workflow imports are in `source_used/`; `source_manifest.json` records the original source-file sizes, public-copy sizes and SHA-256 digests, and corresponding `workflows/` entries. The source code is covered by the upstream licence context retained at the project root.

The preparation chain is: official regional/background drivers and original joint parameters → native land-event forcing and growth/reference calibration → native regional age inputs and secondary gross-carbon requests → N2O mass-corrected complete CMIP7 continuations. Source manifests in the native and secondary bundles preserve the original products, versions, URLs, units and processing identities. Individual scripts expose `--help`; their original input directories and options are source-preparation contracts, not additional model settings.

`workflows/prepare_regional_forest_age_inputs.py --data-root <prepared-source-root> --output <native-input-directory>` performs labelled assembly, not a new fit or simulation. `prepare_secondary_harvest_carbon.py` and `prepare_secondary_harvest_carbon_future.py` construct the native gross-secondary-carbon requests. `prepare_n2o_mass_corrected_inputs.py`, `cmip7_n2o_mass_basis.py` and `prepare_future_history_binding.py` record the atmospheric accounting correction and H0 continuation binding. The complete source workflows remain available for rebuilding from their documented processed sources.

`workflows/build_original_future_drivers.py` reconstructs the original-OSCAR land drivers from the exact five regional products in `inputs/driver_build_sources/` plus the existing CMIP7 atmospheric files. It reads recorded raw-file sizes from the reduction reports. The regional products and atmospheric baseline are included in the prepared-input asset. Repeating the geographical reductions requires the raw products linked in the provider records.
