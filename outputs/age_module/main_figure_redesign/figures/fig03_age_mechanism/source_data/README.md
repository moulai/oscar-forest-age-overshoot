## Mechanism and interpretation details

The local simulations retain normal losses and all tracked carbon pools. Their curves illustrate the prescribed model mechanism and are not independent observational validation. A late positive annual on−off net-uptake difference can reflect lower losses while central NPP remains below off; it does not imply that the earlier cumulative deficit has been recovered. In the full-response global budget, lower actual losses offset 51–58% of the mean production shortfall. The independent actual-loss component tables are source_production_to_net_component_summary.csv and source_production_to_net_paired_member_cumulative_fluxes_all500.csv, with definitions in source_production_to_net_source_definitions.json. These proportions are not age shares of mixed carbon pools or temperature. Complete regional, five-path and original-member sources retain reverse cases.
