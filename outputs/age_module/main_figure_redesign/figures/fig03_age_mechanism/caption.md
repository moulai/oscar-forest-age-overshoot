# English caption

***Fig. 3 | Continual establishment of new forest cohorts sustains the landscape carbon uptake deficit.***

*a, Idealized stand-establishment experiments converting 1 ha of pasture to forest either all at once in year 0 (single) or in equal 0.05 ha yr⁻¹ increments over years 0–19 (staggered), comparing age-dependent production (age on, solid) against the age-invariant reference (age off, dashed) under a fixed 2000–2019 environment. b,c, Annual net primary production (NPP, b) and cumulative net carbon uptake (c) per initial hectare in the Rest of Asia region. Curves show ensemble means, insets show paired differences (age on minus age off) with 5th–95th percentile ranges, and vertical dotted lines mark the completion of staggered planting at year 20. d, Direct effect of age multipliers on NPP across stand age classes in the Very Low (VL) and Low-to-Negative (LN) pathways, averaged over 2023–2099. Bar width, height and area represent mean cohort area, unit-area NPP change and total direct NPP change, with labels reporting the area and NPP-deficit shares of stands younger than 20 years (unaged primary forest carries no age multiplier). e, Ensemble-mean cumulative difference in net land carbon uptake relative to the age-invariant reference when age dependence operates in all cohorts (solid) or only in cohorts already present at the end of 2022 (dashed). Shading and percentages indicate the share of the 2099 deficit attributable to cohorts established after 2022 (n = 500 parameter sets per pathway).*
