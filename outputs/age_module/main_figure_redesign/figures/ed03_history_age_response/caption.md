***Extended Data Fig. 3 | Historical effect of age-dependent production on carbon stocks, atmospheric CO₂ and temperature.***

*Comparison of emissions-driven historical simulations with age-dependent (H1) and age-invariant (H0) stand production. a, Global forest area by stand age class in H1 from 1750 to 2022, with primary forest tracked as an unaged mature class. b, Paired differences in global land carbon stocks in 2022 (H1 minus H0) across total land and individual pools, including differences accumulated between 850 and 1750. Violins show full ensemble distributions, points indicate medians, and thick and thin bars denote interquartile and 5th–95th percentile ranges. c, Phase trajectory of the annual difference in net land carbon uptake against its cumulative difference integrated since 1750. The curve traces the annual ensemble median from 1751 to 2022 and open circles show individual parameter sets in 2022. d, Paired differences in atmospheric CO₂ concentration and global mean surface temperature in 2022. Crosshairs indicate ensemble medians and 5th–95th percentile ranges across the 500 parameter sets, with 98.0% of members exhibiting higher atmospheric CO₂ and higher surface warming in H1. All paired future projections branch from the common H1 state at the end of 2022.*
