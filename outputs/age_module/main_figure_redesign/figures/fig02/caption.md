# English caption

***Fig. 2 | Pre-peak carbon uptake deficits persist through the end of the century despite late-century rate recovery.***

*All differences represent the age-dependent simulation minus the age-invariant reference in the Very Low (VL) and Low-to-Negative (LN) pathways. a, Definition of the four overshoot phases determined for each ensemble member from its age-dependent warming trajectory. b, Mean global forest area by stand age class (years) across the four phases, with unaged primary forest shown separately. c, Phase-averaged annual differences in net land carbon uptake rate (top) and phase-integrated cumulative uptake differences (bottom). Violins show ensemble distributions, and points with vertical bars indicate medians and 5th–95th percentile ranges across members that pass through all four phases (n = 158 in VL and 127 in LN). d, Ensemble-mean phase trajectory of the atmospheric CO₂ difference plotted against the annual net land uptake rate difference from 2023 to 2099, with arrows indicating time progression and green shading marking positive rate differences. e, Cumulative difference in net land carbon uptake over 2022–2099 across the full ensemble, showing medians (lines) and 5th–95th percentile ranges (shading, n = 500 parameter sets per pathway). Net land uptake covers all modelled land classes and living, debris, soil and wood-product pools, excluding permafrost.*
