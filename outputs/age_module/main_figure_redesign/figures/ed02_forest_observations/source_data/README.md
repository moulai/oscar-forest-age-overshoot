# Source Data: forest area, age and living carbon in 2020

## Data sources

1. **Model:** `outputs/age_module/historical_emissions/H1/checkpoints/state_2020.nc`, run identity `5d04b50a41f38dec8d672da070c96bc89ff62d20a3fbe71510b019197d97a3c4`. `rf_A_forest`, `rf_W_forest`, and `rf_F_forest` are summed over secondary/planted classes and actual annual birth cohorts. Primary area/W/F are added separately, without inventing primary ages. `source_config` preserves all 500 original joint-parameter IDs in order.
2. **GAMI v2.1:** [official product and DOI](https://doi.org/10.5880/GFZ.1.4.2023.006), [official versioned description](https://datapub.gfz.de/download/10.5880.GFZ.1.4.2023.006-VEnuo/2023-006_Besnard-et-al_Data-Description-v2.1.pdf). Nominal 2020, twenty realizations. The comparison uses the prepared native-forest histogram, whose support differs from the GAMI/CCI intersection mask.
3. **GLAD forest support:** [official method/definitions](https://glad.umd.edu/dataset/GLCLUC2020). The forest-extent layer requires current tree height of at least 5 m, includes trees outside FAO forest such as agroforestry/orchards, and excludes temporarily unstocked forest. GAMI fuses these disturbance/gain/stable layers with machine-learning age estimates. This differs from the model's land-use forest classes and event clock.
4. **FRA2025:** [official definitions](https://fra-data.fao.org/definitions/fra/2025/en/tad), [official bulk export](https://fra-data.fao.org/api/file/bulk-download?assessmentName=fra&cycleName=2025&countryIso=WO). The comparison uses the 2026-11-05 data snapshot. The 2020 reference rows supply forest area and direct aboveground-plus-belowground carbon; underlying national surveys can have different observation years.

## Statistical and spatial support

`model_regional_members_all500.csv` contains every original member's forest area, living carbon, density and defined age shares, for eleven regions and the global sum. `model_age_composition_all500.csv.gz` preserves every bin/member. Model age is `2020 - birth_time`; bins are [0,20], (20,40], (40,60], (60,80], (80,100], and >100 years. The annual half-year birth support is retained; no real-valued primary age is inferred.

`gami_regional_members_all20.csv` and `gami_age_composition_all20.csv` retain the twenty product realizations. Codes 0-20 include both endpoints; subsequent numerical bins are right-closed. Code 300 is the terminal mature class, not a measured exact age or model primary forest. Code 299 remains unresolved and is included in the total and the native histogram, never silently assigned to a numerical class. Its very small global share is retained in source values even where invisible at the plotted scale. `gami_native_2020_reduced.nc` preserves the full original integer-code histogram. GAMI is inferred from forest inventories, biomass/height, climate and Landsat disturbance information, not direct stand-age observations at all pixels. The documented generation methods do not list LUH/LUH2 as age-estimation inputs; this does not establish absence of every shared upstream source.

Map values are mean regional forest area in Gha. The map geometry in `regional_map_geometry.nc` comes from the existing one-degree OSCAR mask and `reg_code -> IAMC_R10` dictionary. Ocean/unknown code XXX is excluded from the display; regional totals are not reconstructed by counting map pixels. The map assigns the appropriate regional total uniformly to each region solely for visualization. The GAMI geographic reduction uses conditional one-degree region mixtures; these are not exact new country boundaries. FRA aggregates country reports with the same dictionary but is not exactly coextensive, particularly for ROWO, whose model mask also includes disputed territories and lake-region mixtures.

Panel c displays means of source-specific global age shares, so each bar retains its own forest population. In d,e points are medians and lines empirical 5th-95th percentiles. Model members are not paired with GAMI realizations. Model area/age shares have negligible spread under fixed land inputs; their numerical equality must not be called a measure of real-world age uncertainty. Different products/regions are not pooled as independent replicates. No correlation, p value, fit, or combined observational confidence interval is used.

## Carbon and missing-country interpretation

Model forest W+F includes woody and other living tissues, above and below ground, including unaged primary forest. FRA AGC+BGC covers living aboveground and root carbon on forest land. It excludes deadwood, litter, soil and products, as does the plotted model aggregate. FRA treatment of fine roots/understorey can differ from complete model live tissues; this is approximate organ/domain comparability, not exact support matching. CCI woody dry mass was not converted into an artificial full-live-carbon observation.

`fra2025_reported_2020_with_coverage.csv` retains official-country and FAO-imputed counts, available subtotal, complete total, covered forest area and missing-country status. Empty carbon entries are not zero. Incomplete totals are plotted as open diamonds with a right arrow: additional missing nonnegative country carbon has not been estimated; the arrow is not an uncertainty interval. Global available carbon is 311.98817 Pg C, covering 99.98859167% of FRA forest area; a complete global value remains undefined. Coverage in ROWO is 83.7022%; coverage in each other plotted region is at least 99.9307%. ROWO's substantial geography mismatch is separate from missingness. No model rescaling or country-level allocation is attempted.

The model global living-carbon median is 316.39466 Pg C, with empirical 5th-95th percentiles 256.21064-378.13124 Pg C. Its numerical proximity to the **incomplete** FRA subtotal does not establish a same-domain global match and does not cancel regional differences. Europe/India+ model medians are 6.37574/1.31082 Pg C versus FRA available 13.12727/4.23551 Pg C; North America/Rest of Asia are 62.94421/37.45016 versus 42.63183/24.99306 Pg C. Full region values, forest-area differences and own-domain densities are retained in `regional_display_statistics.csv`.

## Data independence and limits

The current model's parameter/input manifest lists host parameters, atmospheric drivers, calibration environments, regional growth curves and wood calibration; this GAMI age map and dated FRA2025 inventory are not direct calibration targets. The age distribution is generated from land-use history, not initialized from GAMI. However, GAMI v2.1 uses ESA-CCI AGB v5 and plot information; growth/input data, host parameters, LUH land history and FRA can share earlier biomass or national-statistics ancestry. That overlap has not been fully resolved. These comparisons therefore assess consistency and identify biases; they are not fully independent verification of future age-production effects, net uptake, CO2 or temperature. They do not validate a global two-year establishment constant.

`manifest.json` gives exact model/product identities and source support. `summary.json` and `regional_display_statistics.csv` hold the displayed summaries.
