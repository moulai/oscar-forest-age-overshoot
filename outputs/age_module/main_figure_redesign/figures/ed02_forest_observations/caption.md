***Extended Data Fig. 2 | Simulated and observed forest area, age structure and living carbon in 2020.***

*a,b, Regional forest area in the emissions-driven historical simulation with age-dependent production (H1, a) and in the GAMI v2.1 remote-sensing product (b). c, Global forest age composition expressed as a fraction of total forest area within each source. Primary forest in OSCAR carries no numerical age, and GAMI v2.1 groups old-growth stands into a mature class without a single numerical age. d, Share of regional forest area aged 20 years or younger in OSCAR and GAMI v2.1. e, Simulated living forest carbon (W + F across primary and secondary forests) compared with above-ground plus below-ground living biomass carbon reported in FAO FRA 2025. Open diamonds with rightward arrows denote regional subtotals where national carbon reporting is incomplete, with reporting countries covering 99.99% of global forest area and 83.7% in the Other region†. Points and error bars in d and e indicate medians and empirical 5th–95th percentile ranges across the 500 OSCAR parameter sets or the 20 GAMI v2.1 realizations. †Countries and territories grouped under Other differ between products.*
