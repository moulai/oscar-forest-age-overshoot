***Extended Data Fig. 1 | Annual forest cohorts in the land carbon cycle of OSCAR.***

*a, Model inputs comprising climate and emissions drivers, gross land-use transitions and wood-harvest demands, and regional biomass–age curves across the 11 land regions. b, Land state and cohort events. Secondary and plantation forests are tracked as annual cohorts defined by their establishment year, area (A), woody carbon (W) and other living tissue (F), whereas primary forest has no numerical age. Forest establishment on non-forest land and harvest of primary forest create new age-zero cohorts, standing harvest in secondary forest removes living carbon while preserving cohort area and birth year, and forest conversion transfers cohort area to non-forest classes. c, Production and turnover pathways. Environmental responses of net primary production, fixed regional partitioning between W and F, and the age multipliers \(g_W(a)\) and \(g_F(a)\) determine cohort production. Mortality and harvest transfer carbon to coarse woody debris (CWD), soil organic carbon and harvested wood products (HWP), which release carbon back to the atmosphere through decomposition, product decay and fire. d, Climate–carbon coupling. Net land carbon uptake modifies atmospheric CO₂, radiative forcing and global surface temperature, which feed back on ecosystem production and turnover losses. Solid arrows denote carbon or area flows and dashed arrows denote process dependencies, with arrow width carrying no quantitative meaning.*
