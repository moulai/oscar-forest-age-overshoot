***Extended Data Fig. 5 | Contributions of pre-2022 and post-2022 cohorts to the warming penalty.***

*a, Two-by-two factorial design in the VL and LN pathways. Age-dependent production is enabled in neither cohort group (00), in cohorts already present at the end of 2022 (10), in cohorts established after 2022 (01) or in both cohort groups (11), with filled circles denoting active age dependence. Factorial contrasts define the main effect of pre-2022 legacy cohorts (\(E_0 = Y_{10} - Y_{00}\)), the direct effect of post-2022 cohorts (\(N_0 = Y_{01} - Y_{00}\)), the conditional effect of post-2022 cohorts (\(N_1 = Y_{11} - Y_{10}\)) and the interaction term (\(I = N_1 - N_0\)). b, Joint distribution of the warming contributions from post-2022 cohorts (\(N_0\)) and pre-2022 cohorts (\(E_0\)) evaluated at the peak warming year of the age-invariant reference run (00) across all 500 parameter sets per pathway. Diamonds mark ensemble means, the dashed diagonal indicates equality (\(N_0 = E_0\)) and inset percentages report the share of members above the diagonal on inverse-hyperbolic-sine (asinh) axes. c, Ensemble-mean growth of the total warming penalty (11 minus 00, solid black line) over the 20 years following the reference peak year, decomposed into additive contributions from post-2022 cohorts (\(N_0\)), pre-2022 cohorts (\(E_0\)) and their interaction (\(I\)), with the dashed curve showing the conditional post-2022 effect (\(N_1\)). Means are evaluated across members whose reference peak occurs as an interior maximum followed by at least 20 simulated years (n = 494 in VL and 491 in LN).*
