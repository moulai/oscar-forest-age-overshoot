***Extended Data Fig. 4 | Effect of age-dependent production on warming across all five CMIP7 pathways.***

*a, Ensemble-median global surface warming relative to 1850–1900 across the High (H), High-to-Low (HL), Medium (M), Very Low (VL) and Low-to-Negative (LN) pathways under age-dependent production (solid lines, with shading indicating the 5th–95th percentile range) and age-invariant production (dashed lines). b, Joint distribution of the paired change in peak warming over 2022–2099 and the paired change in years above 1.5 °C over 2023–2099, formatted as in Fig. 1e. Inset labels report the median change in peak warming and the percentage of members that spend additional years above 1.5 °C, noting that temperature maxima in H and M occur at the 2099 end of the simulation window. c, Proportions of the 500 parameter sets that never exceed 1.5 °C, exceed 1.5 °C and return before 2100, or remain above 1.5 °C in 2099 under age-invariant (left) and age-dependent (right) production. Red ribbons highlight members that lose their pre-2100 return to 1.5 °C, and numeric labels report the percentage of returning members in each configuration (n = 500 parameter sets per pathway).*
